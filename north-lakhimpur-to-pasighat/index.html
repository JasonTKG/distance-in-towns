Distance from North Lakhimpur to Pasighat

The straight-line distance from North Lakhimpur, Assam to Pasighat, Arunachal Pradesh is about 152 km (94 miles). Pasighat is to the north-east of North Lakhimpur.

This is the distance as the crow flies, not by road: the road distance is longer, and this page does not give travel times.

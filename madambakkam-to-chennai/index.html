Distance from Madambakkam to Chennai

The straight-line distance from Madambakkam, Tamil Nadu to Chennai, Tamil Nadu is about 36 km (23 miles). Chennai is to the north-east of Madambakkam.

This is the distance as the crow flies, not by road: the road distance is longer, and this page does not give travel times.
